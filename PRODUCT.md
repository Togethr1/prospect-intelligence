# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Sales representatives and small sales teams researching a prospect immediately before
or during an outbound call. Their primary task is to enter a company website and
receive useful, source-grounded context without copying page content or buying another
data platform.

## Product Purpose

Provide a local-first, open-source, bring-your-own-tools alternative for prospect
research, CRM context, call preparation, and practice. Success means the free default
workflow is useful without API charges, while optional integrations remain explicit,
secure, and user-controlled.

## Brand Personality

Focused, credible, direct. The product should feel like a dependable sales workstation,
not an experimental AI demo.

## Anti-references

Avoid chat-first interfaces, decorative AI gradients, hidden paid actions, fake
integrations, manual copy-and-paste research workflows, and UI that overstates what a
connector can do.

## Design Principles

1. The primary workflow starts with one company URL and does the collection work.
2. Show verified source context and distinguish it from inferred talking points.
3. Keep paid or account-gated behavior opt-in and clearly labeled.
4. Prefer familiar, fast product controls over decorative interaction.
5. Fail safely and explain what the user can do next.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard operation. Respect reduced-motion settings,
never rely on color alone for state, and keep status and error messages available to
assistive technology.
